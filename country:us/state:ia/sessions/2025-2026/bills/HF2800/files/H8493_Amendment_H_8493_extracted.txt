1 Amend the amendment, H-8491, to House File 2800, as follows:
2 1. Page 35, after line 31 by inserting:
3 DIVISION ___
4 GADSDEN FLAG SPECIAL REGISTRATION PLATES
5 Sec. ___. Section 321.34, Code 2026, is amended by adding
6 the following new subsection:
7 NEW SUBSECTION. 11E. Gadsden flag plates.
8 a. Upon application and payment of the proper fees, the
9 director may issue Gadsden flag plates to the owner of a
10 motor vehicle subject to registration under section 321.109,
11 subsection 1, autocycle, motor truck, motor home, multipurpose
12 vehicle, motorcycle, trailer, or travel trailer.
13 b. Gadsden flag plates shall be designed by the department.
14 The background shall be yellow at the top and bottom, with the
15 color fading into white in the center. The plate’s numbers
16 and letters shall be black. The left side of the plate shall
17 depict a black and white rattlesnake coiled on green grass.
18 The image depicting the coiled rattlesnake shall be at least
19 three inches tall and two inches wide. The phrase “Don’t Tread
20 on Me” shall appear at the bottom of the plate.
21 c. The special Gadsden flag fee for letter-number designated
22 Gadsden flag plates is fifty dollars. An applicant may obtain
23 personalized Gadsden flag plates upon payment of the fee for
24 personalized plates as provided in subsection 5, which is in
25 addition to the special Gadsden flag fee. Personalized plates
26 authorized by this subsection shall be limited to no more
27 than five numerals, letters, or combinations of numerals and
28 letters.
29 d. The fees collected by the director under this subsection
30 shall be paid monthly to the treasurer of state and deposited
31 in the road use tax fund. Notwithstanding section 8.33, moneys
32 appropriated under this subsection shall not revert to the
33 general fund of the state.
34 e. Upon receipt of the special registration plates, the
35 applicant shall surrender the current registration plates to
