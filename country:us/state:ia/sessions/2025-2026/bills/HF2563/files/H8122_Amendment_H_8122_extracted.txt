4 <Section 1. PURPOSES. The general assembly finds
5 and declares that the purposes of this Act are all of the
6 following:
7 1. To recognize that the fourteenth amendment to the
8 Constitution of the United States requires that “No state
9 shall...deny to any person within its jurisdiction the equal
10 protection of the laws.”
11 2. To fully recognize that innocent human life, created in
12 the image of God, should be equally protected under the law
13 from fertilization to natural death.
14 3. To ensure that the rights of unborn children are
15 protected from homicide and assault by the same criminal and
16 civil laws protecting all other persons.
17 4. To recognize that all persons potentially subject to such
18 laws are entitled to due process protections.
19 5. To ensure that pregnant women are protected under the law
20 from being pressured to abort a child.
21 6. To remove provisions that may allow a person to solicit,
22 command, aid, or counsel a pregnant woman to abort a child.

31 apportioned by the court among the surviving spouse, children,
