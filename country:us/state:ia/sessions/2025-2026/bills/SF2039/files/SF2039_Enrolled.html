Senate File 2039 - Enrolled Senate File 2039 AN ACT PROVIDING FOR INTERVENTION BY MEMBERS OF THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY IN PROCEEDINGS FOR JUDICIAL REVIEW OF AGENCY ACTION UNDER THE IOWA ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURE ACT. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA: Section 1. NEW SECTION . 17A.19A Intervention by members of the general assembly. The majority leader of the senate, the minority leader of the senate, the speaker of the house of representatives, and the minority leader of the house of representatives may each
Senate File 2039, p. 2 intervene in a proceeding for judicial review of agency action under section 17A.19. Other members of the general assembly may join a motion to intervene filed pursuant to this section. ______________________________ AMY SINCLAIR President of the Senate ______________________________ PAT GRASSLEY Speaker of the House I hereby certify that this bill originated in the Senate and is known as Senate File 2039, Ninety-first General Assembly. ______________________________ W.
