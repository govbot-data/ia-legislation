AN ACT
RELATING TO REQUIREMENTS FOR, AND REGULAR MEETINGS OF, THE BOARD
OF DIRECTORS OF A STATE BANK, AND ELECTRONIC ACTIVITIES OF A
STATE BANK.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA:
Section 1. Section 524.601, subsection 1, Code 2026, is
amended to read as follows:
1. The business and affairs of a state bank shall be managed
by a board of five or more directors eighteen years of age or
older, a majority of whom shall be residents of the state of
Iowa and citizens of the United States, and at least three of
whom shall be residents of the state of Iowa.
Sec. 2. Section 524.607, subsection 1, Code 2026, is amended
to read as follows:
1. The board of directors shall hold at least nine six
regular meetings each calendar year. No more than one
regular meeting shall bo held in any one calendar month At
a minimum, one regular meeting shall be held in each quarter
of a calendar year. A board of directors may be required
to hold additional regular meetings as deemed necessary by
the superintendent. Unless the articles of incorporation or
bylaws provide otherwise, any director may participate in
any meeting of the board of directors through the use of any
means of communication by which all directors participating
may simultaneously hear each other during the meeting. A
