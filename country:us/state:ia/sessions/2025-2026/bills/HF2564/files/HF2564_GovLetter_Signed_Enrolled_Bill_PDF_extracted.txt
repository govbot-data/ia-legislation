House File 2564
AN ACT
RELATING TO A PREGNANT MINOR'S LEGAL CAPACITY TO CONSENT TO THE
PROVISION OF CERTAIN MEDICAL CARE.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA:
Section 1. NEW SECTION . 135.196 Pregnancy care for minors.
1. If a parent, guardian, or legal custodian of a minor
is not reasonably available, the minor shall have the
legal capacity to give consent to the provision of prenatal
care, intrapartum care, or postnatal care to the minor by a
physician, a physician's designee, an advanced registered
nurse practitioner, a physician assistant, a registered nurse,
a licensed practical nurse, or an emergency medical care
provider .

House File 2564, p. 2
2. Subsection 1 shall not be construed to relieve a
physician, a physician's designee, an advanced registered nurse
practitioner, a physician assistant, a registered nurse, a
licensed practical nurse, or an emergency medical care provider
of the duty to obtain informed consent from the minor patient
for the provision of such care.
