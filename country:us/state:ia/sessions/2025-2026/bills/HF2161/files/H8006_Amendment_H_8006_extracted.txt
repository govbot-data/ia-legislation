4 <Section 1. Section 321P.4, Code 2026, is amended by adding
5 the following new subsections:
6 NEW SUBSECTION. 01. A person shall not use an automatic
7 registration plate reader in a local authority unless
8 the governing body of the local authority has adopted an
9 ordinance authorizing the use of a plate reader in the local
10 authority. The ordinance must provide the name or employment
11 classification of persons authorized to access a plate reader.
12 NEW SUBSECTION. 001. a. A person shall not access an
13 automatic registration plate reader to perform a search unless
14 the search is related to a call for service or case and the
15 call for service or case is identified by an assigned specific
16 number. However, if exigent circumstances exist and a specific
17 number has not been assigned, a person may search a plate
18 reader without providing a call for service number or case
19 number at the time of the search, and the person shall add the
20 call for service number or case number, as applicable, after
21 the number is assigned.
22 b. A person who accesses a plate reader shall record the
23 person’s access in a records log maintained by the local
24 authority or in the plate reader’s vendor-generated audit
25 logs. The log must include the call for service number or case
26 number, as applicable, and the reason for accessing the plate
27 reader.
28 NEW SUBSECTION. 0001. a. A person shall not add a
29 registration plate to a database if the database includes
30 specific registration plates for which an automatic
31 registration plate reader is designed to provide an alert
32 when the plate reader identifies a registration plate from
33 the database, unless the person provides the call for service
34 number or case number in the database entry.
35 b. A person shall not rely on an alert from a plate reader

3 NEW SUBSECTION. 2A. A person shall not provide a copy
4 of an image of a vehicle registration plate, or any other

13 c. The national insurance crime bureau or its successor

17 claims.
18 NEW SUBSECTION. 2B. A person shall not use an automatic
19 registration plate reader that is able to recognize or identify
20 a person based on a photograph or recording of the person’s
21 face captured by the plate reader. A person who uses a plate
22 reader shall not use software designed to recognize or identify
23 a person based on a photograph or recording of the person’s
24 face captured by the plate reader.
25 NEW SUBSECTION. 2C. A person shall not place an automatic
26 registration plate reader directed at a church, synagogue, or
27 other established place of religious worship, unless the person
28 has consent from the affected church, synagogue, or other
29 established place of religious worship, as applicable.
30 NEW SUBSECTION. 2D. The governing body of a local authority
31 that authorized the use of an automatic registration plate
32 reader shall audit or cause an audit of the use of each plate
33 reader within the local authority at least once every three
34 months. The governing body of the local authority shall
35 document the audit.
