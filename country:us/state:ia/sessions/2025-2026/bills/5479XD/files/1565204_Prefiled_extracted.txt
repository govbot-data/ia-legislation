18 EXPLANATION
19 The inclusion of this explanation does not constitute agreement with
20 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
21 This bill relates to prohibited activities in the
22 administration of alcoholic beverage control by the department
23 of revenue (IDR).
24 The bill specifies that not more than two members of the
25 alcoholic beverages commission (member) shall directly or
26 indirectly have an interest in alcohol-related enterprises,
27 except as institutional investors. The bill prohibits a member
28 from influencing, persuading, or inducing others to adopt
29 political views of the member or favor any particular elective
30 or appointive candidates.
31 The bill prohibits the director of revenue from having
32 any interest in alcohol-related enterprises, except as an
33 institutional investor.
34 The bill prohibits an IDR employee from licensing,
35 permitting, or participating in the licensing or permitting of
