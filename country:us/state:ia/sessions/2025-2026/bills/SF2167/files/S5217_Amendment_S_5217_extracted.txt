4 <Section 1. NEW SECTION. 321.175 Human trafficking
5 prevention training —— commercial drivers.
6 1. An applicant for the initial issuance of a class A,
7 B, or C commercial driver’s license must certify to the
8 department that the applicant successfully completed a course
9 of instruction regarding human trafficking prevention.
10 2. A course of instruction regarding human trafficking
11 prevention must last at least thirty minutes and include
12 training based on the department’s approved human trafficking
13 prevention training materials. The course must be offered in
14 the English language.
15 3. The department shall approve, in cooperation with the
16 department of education and the state board of education,
17 human trafficking prevention training materials regarding the
18 recognition, prevention, and reporting of human trafficking.
19 The departments may collaborate with other entities that
20 specialize in and produce training materials regarding the
21 recognition, prevention, and reporting of human trafficking
22 particularly for persons who operate commercial motor vehicles.
23 The departments shall make the approved human trafficking
24 prevention training materials available to the public. The
25 departments shall review and update the departments’ approved
26 human trafficking prevention training materials to account for
27 changes and trends in human trafficking at least once every
28 three years.
29 4. The department may adopt rules pursuant to chapter 17A to
30 administer this section.
