21 EXPLANATION
22 The inclusion of this explanation does not constitute agreement with
23 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
24 This bill relates to controlled substances, including
25 certain controlled substances schedules.
26 The bill makes changes to the controlled substances schedule
27 I in Iowa’s uniform controlled substances Act (Code chapter
28 124) to conform to the specific chemical designations of
29 the substances contained in Title 21 of the Code of Federal
30 Regulations, part 1308.
31 A violation involving a controlled substance included in the
32 bill may result in a class “B” felony, and in addition to the
33 25-year mandatory minimum term of imprisonment for class “B”
34 felons, a fine of not less than $5,000 nor more than $100,000;
35 a class “C” felony punishable by imprisonment up to 10 years
