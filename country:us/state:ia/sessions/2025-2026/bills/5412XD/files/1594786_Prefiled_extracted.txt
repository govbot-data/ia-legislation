6 CHAUFFEUR’S INSTRUCTION PERMITS. Under current law, a
7 chauffeur’s instruction permit is valid for up to two years
8 for the operation of a motor vehicle, other than a commercial
9 motor vehicle, as a chauffeur when the permittee is accompanied
10 by a person who possesses a valid class D driver’s license
11 (chauffeur’s license) or commercial driver’s license and who
12 is occupying a seat beside the permittee. Code section 321.1
13 defines a chauffeur as a person who operates a motor vehicle,
14 including a school bus, to transport people for pay, with
15 various exceptions.
16 The bill eliminates the chauffeur’s instruction permit.
17 Under current law and the bill, a person may apply for a class
18 D driver’s license, and neither a knowledge test nor a driving
19 test is required if the person holds a valid driver’s license.
20 The person must pass a vision screening and have a clear
21 driving record for the previous two years.
