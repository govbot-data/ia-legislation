14 Sec. 4. NEW SECTION. 124F.4 Adverse health events.
15 1. If a processor or the department receives notice of any
16 adverse health event suspected to be related to the processor’s
17 kratom product, the processor or the department shall submit an
18 adverse event report, as set out in the Federal Food, Drug, and
19 Cosmetic Act, 21 U.S.C. §379aa-1(b)(1), to the United States
20 food and drug administration.
21 2. If probable cause exists that a kratom product may
22 be adulterated, the department may require an independent
23 third-party test of the kratom product by a laboratory of the
24 department’s choice, and the processor shall pay the cost of
25 the test. If the processor does not make such payment to the
26 department within thirty days after receiving the invoice for
27 the testing fee, the department shall revoke the registration
28 for that product.
