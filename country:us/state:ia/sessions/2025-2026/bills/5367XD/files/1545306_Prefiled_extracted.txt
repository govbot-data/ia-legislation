1 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
2 This bill relates to sentencing of public employees and
3 public officials for certain offenses.
4 The bill prohibits a sentencing court from deferring the
5 judgment or deferring the sentence of a defendant if the
6 defendant was a public employee or public official at the time
7 of the offense and the amount of money taken by the public
8 employee or public official in connection with the offense
9 exceeded $10,000.
10 The bill prohibits a sentencing court from suspending
11 the sentence of a defendant if the defendant was a public
12 employee or public official at the time of the offense and
13 the amount of money taken by the public employee or public
14 official in connection with the offense exceeded $10,000 unless
15 the defendant establishes, by clear and convincing evidence,
16 and the sentencing court finds and states specifically on
17 the record, that mitigating circumstances exist warranting
18 a departure from the otherwise mandatory minimum term of
19 incarceration.
20 The bill defines “public employee” as any individual
21 employed by a public employer and includes a contractor
22 or vendor; “public employer” as the state, its boards,
23 commissions, agencies, and departments, and its political
24 subdivisions including school districts and other special
25 purpose districts; and “public official” as officials, local
26 officials, and members of the general assembly (as defined in
27 Code section 68B.2).
