20 EXPLANATION
21 The inclusion of this explanation does not constitute agreement with
22 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
23 This bill relates to the administration of the utilities
24 commission by creating the position of executive director
25 and modifying the duties and titles of the chairperson and
26 executive staff.
27 The bill specifies the chairperson’s duties regarding
28 presiding over meetings and proceedings.
29 The bill replaces the position of chief operating officer
30 with executive director, and makes conforming changes by
31 replacing references of a chief operating officer with
32 executive director. The bill states that the commission
33 shall appoint the executive director by a majority vote,
34 the executive director will serve at the pleasure of the
35 commission, and the executive director will be overseen and
