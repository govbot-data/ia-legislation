23 EXPLANATION
24 The inclusion of this explanation does not constitute agreement with
25 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
26 This bill relates to the commencement of audits, information
27 made available to the auditor of state, and disputes between
28 governmental agencies. The bill strikes or repeals provisions
29 enacted by the general assembly in 2023 Iowa Acts, chapter 103
30 (SF 478).
31 Specifically, the bill strikes prohibitions on the receipt
32 and disclosure of certain information by the auditor of state
33 except under certain circumstances, and makes conforming
34 changes. The bill also strikes a requirement that litigation
35 between state constitutional and statutory offices be submitted
