Senate File 274
AN ACT
RELATING TO FORMS OF PAYMENT FOR ENTRY INTO AN EXTRACURRICULAR
INTERSCHOLASTIC ATHLETIC CONTEST OR COMPETITION.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA:
Section 1. NEW SECTION. 280.13H Extracurricular
interscholastic athletic contests or competitions — acceptance
of legal tender required.
1. For purposes of this section, "organization" means the
same as defined in section 280.13.
2. A public school shall accept legal tender as a form of
payment for entry into any extracurricular interscholastic
athletic contest or competition sponsored by an organization.
As a condition of registration with the department of education
under section 280.13, an organization shall accept legal
tender as a form of payment for entry into any extracurricular
interscholastic athletic contest or competition sponsored by
the organization.
3. This section shall not apply to any of the following:
a. An extracurricular interscholastic athletic contest or
competition that takes place at a public school or a nonpublic
school that is not a member of an organization.

Senate File 274, p. 2
b. An extracurricular interscholastic athletic contest or
competition, sponsored by an organization, that takes place at
a venue that is not owned or operated by a public school, a
nonpublic school or an organization.
