17 EXPLANATION
18 The inclusion of this explanation does not constitute agreement with
19 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
20 This bill relates to criminal investigations. The bill
21 extends DNA submission requirements to persons arrested for
22 a felony or aggravated misdemeanor, and provides a standing
23 appropriation to support the investigation of cold cases.
24 DIVISION I. Current law requires a person who receives a
25 deferred judgment for a felony or against whom a judgment or
26 conviction for a felony or aggravated misdemeanor has been
27 entered to submit a DNA sample for DNA profiling.
28 The bill provides that a person who is arrested for a felony
29 or aggravated misdemeanor, or who receives a deferred judgment
30 for a felony or against whom a judgment or conviction for a
31 felony or aggravated misdemeanor has been entered, shall be
32 required to submit a DNA sample for DNA profiling pursuant to
33 Code section 81.4.
34 The bill provides that a sample is not required if it is
35 determined that a sample has previously been taken, is in the
