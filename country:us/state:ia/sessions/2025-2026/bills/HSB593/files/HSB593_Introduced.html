H.F. _____ This bill relates to the purchase of lottery shares and 1 tickets by a courier service. The bill allows a courier 2 service, defined in the bill as a person that purchases and 3 stores a share or ticket on behalf of a customer and delivers 4 the share or ticket, or the prize from the share or ticket, to 5 the customer as a for-profit service, to enter into a written 6 agreement with a lottery retailer to purchase, store, and 7 deliver shares, tickets, and prizes on behalf of customers 8 located in this state. 9 The bill specifies certain terms that a written agreement 10 between a courier service and a retailer must contain. These 11 include terms for verifying the location and age of a customer 12 making a purchase through a courier service, a requirement 13 that a share or ticket be securely stored by a courier service 14 before being picked up by or delivered to the customer or 15 redeemed by the courier service for the purpose of pickup or 16 delivery of the prize to the customer, a requirement that a 17 courier service maintain certain responsible play controls 18 and comply with advertising guidelines, a requirement that a 19 courier service only purchase shares or tickets at a dedicated 20 terminal or self-service kiosk, a provision allowing a courier 21 service to charge reasonable fees to customers, a prohibition 22 on the purchase of shares or tickets using a credit card, and 23 a requirement that a courier service annually contract for an 24 audit of the courier serviceâs compliance with the written 25 agreement. Such agreements must be disclosed by the retailer 26 to the administrator of the Iowa lottery within three business 27 days after execution. 28 The bill requires the Iowa lottery commission to issue 29 dedicated self-service terminals or kiosks for use by courier 30 services and prohibits a retailer from commingling retail share 31 and ticket sales with sales to courier services. 32 -3- LSB 5930YC (4) 91 ss/ns 3/ 3
