13 EXPLANATION
14 The inclusion of this explanation does not constitute agreement with
15 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
16 This bill relates to natural resources, including office
17 locations for the director of the department of natural
18 resources, state park user fee pilot programs, the delegation
19 of powers and duties concerning state preserves, and age
20 requirements for hunting deer with pistols or revolvers.
21 The bill removes references to the Lake Manawa and Waubonsie
22 state park user fee pilot programs that repealed by their own
23 terms on December 31, 2025.
24 Under current law, the director of the department of natural
25 resources (DNR) must maintain an office at the state capitol.
26 The bill allows the office to be at or near the state capitol.
27 Under current law, DNR has certain powers and duties
28 regarding state preserves. The bill transfers all these powers
29 and duties to the natural resource commission.
30 Under current law, a person who is 20 years of age of less
31 is not allowed to hunt deer with a pistol or revolver unless
32 accompanied by person who is at least 21 years of age with
33 a valid hunting license and with the consent of a parent,
34 guardian, or spouse who is at least 21 years of age. The bill
35 changes the age threshold from 20 years of age or less to under
