GENERAI. ASSEMBLY
House File 2720
AN ACT
RELATING TO DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE, INCLUDING NAME CHANGE
REQUESTS.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA:
Section 1. Section 598.37, Code 2026, is amended to read as
follows :
598.37 Name change.
1. a. Either party to a marriage may request as a part of
the decree of dissolution or decree of annulment a change in
the person's name to either the name appearing on the person's
birth certificate or to the name the person had immediately
prior to the marriage.
b. If a party requests a name change other than to the name
appearing on the person's birth certificate or to the name the
person had immediately prior to the marriage, the request shall
be made under chapter 674.

House File 2720, p. 2
2. If a party requests a name change under subsection
1, paragraph "a", the court shall issue a separate document
evidencing the name change. The document shall not contain
any other information relating to the decree of dissolution or
decree of annulment.,
