Senate File 2430
AN ACT
RELATING TO THE DISPLAY OF FLAGS AT HALF-STAFF ON PUBLIC
BUILDINGS.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA:
Section 1. Section IB. 3, Code 2026, is amended to read as
follows :
IB. 3 Flags on public buildings.
1. As used in this section, "public building" means a
vertical improvement, as defined in section 15J.2, used for a
public purpose by the state or a political subdivision of the
state.
2. It shall be the duty of any A board of public officers
charged with providing supplies for a public building in the
state te shall provide a suitable state flag^ and Et- shall be
the duty of the custodian of that public building -fee shall
raise the flags of the United States of America and the state
of Iowa— upon each secular day when weather conditions are
favorable .
3. The custodian and the board of public officers of a
public building in the state shall have the flags of the United
States of America and the state of Iowa flown at half-staff
when so directed by a proclamation issued by the governor.
4. A person may report an alleged violation of subsection 3
to the attorney general. The attorney general may initiate an
action in the name of the state, including but not limited

Senate File 2430, p. 2
to an action for injunctive relief to obtain compliance with
subsection 3.
