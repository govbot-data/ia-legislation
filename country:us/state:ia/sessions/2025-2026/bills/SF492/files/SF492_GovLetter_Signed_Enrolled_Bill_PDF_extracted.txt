BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA:
Section 1. Section 321.52, subsection 2, paragraph b, Code
2025, is amended to read as follows:
b. The owner of a motor vehicle subject to registration
that who does not have a certificate of title or a junking
certificate may dispose of the vehicle to a vehicle recycler
licensed under chapter 321H for scrap or junk if the vehicle is
twelve model years old or older and is acquired by the vehicle
recycler for reasonable consideration equaling less than one
thousand dollars. A licensed vehicle recycler shall not
acquire such a vehicle unless the recycler uses the recycler's
access to the state records system described in section 321.31
to verify there is not a lienholder who has a security interest
in the vehicle. However, a licensed vehicle recycler is not
required to verify the nonexistence of a lienholder if the
