House File 2485
AN ACT
RELATING TO MOTOR VEHICLE DEALERSr INCLUDING RIGHTS TO CERTAIN
VEHICLES, RECORDS STORAGE REQUIREMENTS, AND REMOTE SALES OF
MOTOR VEHICLES.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA:
Section 1. Section 321.45, subsection 2, Code 2026, is
amended by adding the following new paragraph:
NEW PARAGRAPH, c. For purposes of this subsection, a motor
vehicle dealer licensed under chapter 322 shall be deemed to
have acquired and to possess the right, title, claim, and
interest in a vehicle if the vehicle's certificate of title is
issued or properly assigned to an entity with direct ownership
or control over the dealer.
Sec. 2. Section 321.63, Code 2026, is amended by adding the
following new subsection:
NEW SUBSECTION, 3. A dealer shall do all of the following:
a. Store all records at a place of business of the dealer
or at a place of business within the United States of an entity
that has direct ownership or control over the dealer.
b» Provide electronic records to the department within
twenty-four hours after receiving a request.
c. Provide physical records to the department within a
reasonable time considering postal delivery.
Sec. 3. Section 322.3, subsection 12, paragraphs a and c.
Code 2026, are amended to read as follows:

NEW PARAGRAPH, f. The dealer shall disclose to a buyer all

(1) That the vehicle is not located in this state, if
