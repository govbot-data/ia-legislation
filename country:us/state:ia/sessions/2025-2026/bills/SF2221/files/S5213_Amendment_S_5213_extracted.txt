4 <Section 1. Section 256.146, Code 2026, is amended by adding
5 the following new subsections:
6 NEW SUBSECTION. 28. a. Adopt rules pursuant to chapter 17A
7 establishing additional endorsements for instruction related
8 to all of the following high-need teaching areas in schools
9 providing instruction to students enrolled in prekindergarten
10 through grade twelve:
11 (1) Fine arts.
12 (2) Mathematics.
13 (3) Science.
14 b. The board shall not grant an applicant an endorsement
15 described in paragraph “a” unless the applicant holds a
16 bachelor’s degree from an accredited college or university.
17 c. This subsection is repealed July 1, 2030.
18 NEW SUBSECTION. 29. Adopt rules pursuant to chapter 17A
19 establishing an additional kindergarten through grade twelve
20 instructional strategist II endorsement that does not require
21 the applicant to possess a master’s or doctoral degree as a
22 condition of obtaining the endorsement.
23 NEW SUBSECTION. 30. Adopt rules pursuant to chapter 17A
24 establishing an activities administration authorization.
25 The rules must require an applicant for the activities
26 administration authorization to hold a baccalaureate degree or
27 higher in any academic field.>
28 2. Title page, by striking lines 1 through 3 and inserting
29 <An Act requiring the board of educational examiners to
30 establish additional endorsements and authorizations.>
