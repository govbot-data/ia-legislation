1 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
2 Under current law, the state public defender (SPD) may
3 establish a pilot project to implement innovative models
4 of legal representation to assist families involved in the
5 child welfare system by implementing and studying ways to
6 achieve positive outcomes for families, reduce trauma to young
7 children, and deliver financial benefits to families and their
8 communities. The SPD may implement the pilot project in up to
9 16 counties throughout the state, and the pilot project expires
10 on June 30, 2025.
11 This bill changes the pilot project into a permanent program
12 known as the project to preserve families (PPF) and allows the
13 SPD to administer and maintain the PPF throughout the state.
14 The SPD may adopt rules to administer the PPF.
