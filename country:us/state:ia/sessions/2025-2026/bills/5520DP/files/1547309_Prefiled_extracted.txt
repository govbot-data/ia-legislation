8 EXPLANATION
9 The inclusion of this explanation does not constitute agreement with
10 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
11 This bill relates to utility disconnections of gas and
12 electricity services during severe weather.
13 The bill adds a requirement for the Iowa utilities
14 commission (commission) to adopt rules that prohibit the
15 disconnection of gas or electric service to a residence during
16 periods of severe cold weather or severe hot weather, as
17 defined by such rules. The commission currently has rules to
18 prohibit the disconnection of gas or electric service under
19 certain conditions relating to severe cold weather.
