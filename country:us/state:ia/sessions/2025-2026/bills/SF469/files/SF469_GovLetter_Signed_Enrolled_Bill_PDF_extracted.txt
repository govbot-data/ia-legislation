Senate File 469
AN ACT
CREATING AN EMERITUS LICENSE TO PRACTICE MEDICINE AND SURGERY OR
OSTEOPATHIC MEDICINE AND SURGERY.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA:
Section 1. NEW SECTION. 148.4 Emeritus license.
1. The board shall grant an emeritus license to a physician
and surgeon or osteopathic physician and surgeon who is
at least sixty years of age, who is or will be primarily
engaged in the practice of supervising and training resident
physicians, and who submits an application on a form prescribed
by the board.
2. An emeritus license issued pursuant to this section shall
entitle the licensee to maintain the same scope of practice
as the licensee had as a physician and surgeon or osteopathic
physician and surgeon.
3. An emeritus license issued pursuant to this section shall
be valid for three years from the date of issue and may be
renewed once.
4. Notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary, a
licensee licensed pursuant to this section is not required to
complete continuing education credits to maintain or renew an
emeritus license.
5. The application and renewal fee for an emeritus license
shall be one half of the application and renewal fees for
a license to practice medicine and surgery or osteopathic
medicine and surgery.

Senate File 469, p. 2
6. The board shall adopt rules for the implementation
of this section, including a rule that defines "primarily
engaged in the practice of supervising and training resident
physicians" .
7. The board shall not issue a license pursuant to this
section prior to January 1 2026.
