Senate File 2219
AN ACT
REQUIRING BOARDS OF DIRECTORS OF SCHOOL DISTRICTS, GOVERNING
BOARDS OF CHARTER SCHOOLS, AND AUTHORITIES IN CHARGE OF
ACCREDITED NONPUBLIC SCHOOLS TO ADOPT POLICIES RELATED TO
EXEMPTING STUDENT ABSENCES FOR SCHOOL-SPONSORED ACTIVITIES,
PROGRAMS, COMPETITIONS, AND PROJECTS.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA:
Section 1. Section 256E.7, subsection 2, Code 2026, is
amended by adding the following new paragraph:
NEW PARAGRAPH. Ofir, Be subject to and comply with the
requirements of section 299.1, subsection 5, relating
to absences from school because of involvement in a
school-sponsored activity, program, competition, or project in
the same manner as a school district.
Sec. 2. Section 299.1, Code 2026, is amended by adding the
following new subsection:
NEW SUBSECTION. 5. The board of directors of each public
school district and the governing body of each accredited
nonpublic school shall adopt a policy that requires the school
district or the accredited nonpublic school to grant a student
an exempt absence when the student is absent from school
because of the student's involvement in a school-sponsored
activity, program, competition, or project. The policy shall
also require the school district or accredited nonpublic school
to allow a student who is absent from school because of the
student's involvement in a school-sponsored activity, program.
