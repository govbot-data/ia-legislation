1 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
2 This bill relates to teacher education and licensing,
3 including by modifying provisions related to practitioner
4 preparation programs and licenses issued by the board of
5 educational examiners (BOEE).
6 Beginning July 1, 2027, the bill requires higher education
7 institutions providing practitioner preparation to include
8 clinical experiences and preparation to prepare students
9 admitted to a practitioner preparation program to serve
10 students with disabilities and English learners. The rules
11 adopted by the state board pursuant to this provision must
12 prescribe practicum and clinical experience standards for
13 practitioner preparation programs related to serving students
14 with disabilities and English learners. Clinical experiences
15 for all students are required to include a minimum of 15
16 practicum hours focused on differentiation of instruction
17 and support for special populations. Meanwhile, clinical
18 experiences for students who are seeking an endorsement in
19 English learner instruction must include a minimum of 40
20 practicum hours, inclusive of methods of teaching English
21 to English learners in both elementary and secondary school
22 settings. The bill requires the department of education
23 (DE) to establish a process to review and provide ongoing
24 support for the adoption and implementation of standards for
25 preparation of teacher and administrator candidates in serving
26 students with disabilities and English learners.
27 Beginning July 1, 2027, the bill requires higher education
28 institutions providing practitioner preparation to administer
29 prior to a student’s completion of the practitioner preparation
30 program a summative end-of-program assessment that is designed
31 to measure the student’s preparation in content areas that are
32 required for the endorsement area in which the student will
33 be teaching, and that was distributed to the higher education
34 institution by DE pursuant to the bill’s provisions.
35 The bill requires DE to, on or before July 1, 2027, identify
