5 EXPLANATION
6 The inclusion of this explanation does not constitute agreement with
7 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
8 This bill relates to programs and procedures of the
9 department of workforce development (department).
10 DIVISION I —— REEMPLOYMENT CASE MANAGEMENT PROGRAM. The
11 bill requires the department, as a required part of its
12 administration of unemployment compensation benefits, to
13 operate a reemployment case management program. The program
14 shall provide individualized reemployment services to claimants
15 who are no longer attached to work. The bill specifies the
16 time period for the services. The bill provides that an
17 individual is considered to be earnestly and actively seeking
18 work for purposes of eligibility for benefits if the individual
19 is participating in and compliant with the program.
20 DIVISION II —— UNEMPLOYMENT BENEFITS —— MEANS OF
21 COMMUNICATION. The bill replaces various references to
22 communication by mail with communication in the department’s
23 online portal or by electronic mail. The references relate
24 to determination of eligibility for unemployment benefits and
25 determination of employer contribution rates for such benefits.
26 The bill authorizes the director of the department to
27 establish by rule an alternate method or methods for employers
28 and individuals to provide required information relating to
29 unemployment benefits to the department and for the department
30 to provide required notifications to an employer or an
31 individual in circumstances when it is established that the
32 employer or individual is unable to provide the information
33 to the department by electronic means or is unable to receive
34 notifications from the department by electronic means.
