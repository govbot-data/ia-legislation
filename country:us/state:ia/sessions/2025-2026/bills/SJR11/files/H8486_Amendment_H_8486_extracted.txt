Senate Joint Resolution 11
H-8486
1 Amend Senate Joint Resolution 11, as passed by the Senate, as
2 follows:
3 1. By striking everything after the resolving clause and
4 inserting:
5 <Section 1. The following amendment to the Constitution of
6 the State of Iowa is proposed:
7 The Constitution of the State of Iowa is amended by adding
8 the following new section to new Article XIII:
9 ARTICLE XIII.
10 EDUCATION SAVINGS ACCOUNTS —— LIMITATIONS.
11 Section 1. Two-thirds majority vote for education savings
12 account changes. Passage of a bill that increases funding for
13 the education savings account program or any equivalent program
14 shall require the affirmative votes of at least two-thirds of
15 the members elected to each house of the general assembly.
16 A lawsuit challenging the proper enactment of a bill under
17 this section must be filed no later than one year following the
18 enactment. If such a lawsuit is not filed within the one-year
19 limit, the bill shall be considered properly enacted under this
20 section.
21 Each bill to which this section applies must include a
22 separate provision describing the requirements for enactment
23 prescribed by this section.
24 The general assembly shall enact laws to implement this
25 section.
26 Sec. 2. REFERRAL AND PUBLICATION. The foregoing proposed
27 amendment to the Constitution of the State of Iowa is referred
28 to the general assembly to be chosen at the next general
29 election for members of the general assembly, and shall be
30 published as provided by law for three months previous to the
31 date of that election.>
32 2. Title page, by striking line 3 and inserting <education
33 savings accounts funding increases.>
