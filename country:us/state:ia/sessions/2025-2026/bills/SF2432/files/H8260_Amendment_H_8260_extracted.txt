4 Sec. ___. Section 336.10, Code 2026, is amended by adding
5 the following new subsection:
6 NEW SUBSECTION. 3. A library shall not receive moneys from
7 this fund if the library is in violation of the age-appropriate
8 policies described in section 8A.209.
9 Sec. ___. NEW SECTION. 336.20 Age-appropriate material.
10 1. a. The board of library trustees shall adopt and enforce
11 age-appropriate policies for the library, as described in
12 section 8A.209. Such policies shall apply to any mobile and
13 temporary services, events, and retailers on library property.
14 Any violations of a policy that are not remedied within thirty
15 days shall constitute a violation of law and may be referred
16 to the county attorney. A library that is in violation of
17 age-appropriate policies and requirements is not eligible to
18 receive public funds.
19 b. If a library enters an agreement pursuant to chapter 28E
20 for the purpose of providing library services to a school, such
21 agreement shall provide that the age-appropriate policies of
22 the school will be enforced. Parental permissions granted to
23 a minor for the library, pursuant to subsection 2, shall not
24 apply to the student during school use of the library.
25 2. The library board shall review all library materials to
26 determine if the materials are age-appropriate, as defined in
27 section 8A.209, subsection 4, paragraph “d”. Material deemed
28 inappropriate for minors shall not be accessible to minors
29 unless parental approval has been given for each specific item.
30 3. The board shall establish a procedure for a citizen
31 residing in the library’s service area to challenge the board’s
32 determination that an item is age-appropriate. If a hearing is
33 held, the board must allow three minutes of public comment for
34 each person wishing to speak and up to thirty minutes total of
35 public comment.

3 1. The board of library trustees shall publish all of the

23 prominently displayed on the library’s internet site and the
24 board of library trustees shall, at least annually, provide
25 a written or electronic copy of the policy to the parent or
26 guardian of each library patron who is a minor.
27 3. The identity of a parent or guardian who requests the
28 removal of material that is available to library patrons who

30 be confidential and shall not be a public record subject to

1 Sec. ___. NEW SECTION. 336.22 Appeal —— governing body
2 meeting agenda —— public comment.
3 1. Upon appeal of a decision of the board of trustees of
4 the library filed with the governing body, the governing body
5 shall place the library board decision specified in the appeal
6 on the agenda of the next regular meeting of the governing body
7 or on the agenda of the governing body meeting held within
8 thirty days of receipt of the appeal filed in accordance with
9 this subsection. If the decision relates to age-appropriate
10 material, the governing body may halt access to the subject
11 materials until the governing body holds the meeting at which
12 the decision is presented and discussed. The meeting notice
13 shall include a brief description of the decision.
14 2. The governing body shall provide sufficient time to
15 receive public comment on the decision. The governing body
16 shall allow each interested member of the public to speak at
17 the meeting regarding the decision, but may impose a time limit
18 on the amount of time a member of the public is allowed to speak
19 if the time limit is the same for each speaker and necessary
20 due to the amount of people wishing to speak.
21 Sec. ___. NEW SECTION. 395.1 City libraries —— oversight by
22 city council.
23 1. The city council has all administrative, financial,
24 oversight, and policy authority of any city library under the
25 city’s jurisdiction.
26 2. The city shall enter an agreement pursuant to chapter
27 28E with any friends of the library group or similar nonprofit
28 organization existing in the city. The purpose of the
29 agreement is to assist the library in ensuring enforcement of
30 policies of the library.
31 3. The city council may appoint a library director. A
32 candidate for library director shall submit an application. A
33 candidate’s application shall be a public record. A public
34 hearing shall be held on the potential hiring before the city
35 council votes to appoint a library director.
