House File 992 - Enrolled House File 992 AN ACT RELATING TO THE FEE FOR DUPLICATES OR OTHER EVIDENCE OF INTERSTATE FUEL USE TAX PERMITS OR LICENSES. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA: Section 1. Section 452A.53, subsection 5, Code 2025, is amended to read as follows: 5. Each vehicle operated into or through Iowa in interstate operations using motor fuel, special fuel, or electric fuel acquired in any other state shall carry in or on the vehicle a duplicate or evidence of the permit or license required in this section. A fee not to exceed fifty cents one dollar shall House File 992, p. 2 be charged for each duplicate or other evidence of a permit or license issued. ______________________________ PAT GRASSLEY Speaker of the House ______________________________ AMY SINCLAIR President of the Senate I hereby certify that this bill originated in the House and is known as House File 992, Ninety-first General Assembly. ______________________________ MEGHAN NELSON Chief Clerk of the House Approved _______________, 2026 ______________________________ KIM REYNOLDS Governor
