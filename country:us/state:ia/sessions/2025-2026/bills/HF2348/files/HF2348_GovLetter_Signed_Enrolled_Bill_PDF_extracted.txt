House File 2348
AN ACT
RELATING TO ANIMAL TORTURE, AND PROVIDING PENALTIES.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA:
Section 1, Section 717B.3A, Code 2026, is amended by adding
the following new subsection:
NEW SUBSECTION. lA. A person is guilty of animal torture
if the person intentionally, willfully, and maliciously
mutilates, burns, poisons, drowns, starves, or causes intensive
or prolonged pain or death to a companion animal, or causes,
directs, or provides anything of value to another person to do
the same.
Sec. 2. Section 717B.3A, subsections 4 and 5, Code 2026, are
amended to read as follows:
4. A person who commits animal torture in violation of
subsection 1 is guilty of an aggravated misdemeanor. A person
who commits animal torture in violation of subsection lA is
guilty of a class ^^D*^ felony.

animal torture is guilty of a claoo ^^D^^ felony shall be guilty
