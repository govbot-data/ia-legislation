6 Section 1. GOALS AND ACCOUNTABILITY —— ECONOMIC
7 DEVELOPMENT.
8 1. For the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2026, the goals
9 for the economic development authority shall be to expand
10 and stimulate the state economy, increase the wealth of
11 Iowans, increase the population of the state, and cultivate an
12 inclusive and welcoming environment for all citizens of Iowa.
13 2. To achieve the goals in subsection 1, the economic
14 development authority shall do all of the following for the
15 fiscal year beginning July 1, 2026:
16 a. Concentrate its efforts on programs and activities that
17 result in commercially viable products and services.
18 b. Adopt practices and services consistent with free
19 market, private sector philosophies.
20 c. Ensure economic growth and development throughout the
21 state.
22 d. Work with businesses and communities to continually
23 improve the economic development climate along with the
24 economic well-being and quality of life for Iowans.
25 e. Coordinate with other state agencies to ensure that they
26 are attentive to the needs of an entrepreneurial culture.
27 f. Establish a strong and aggressive marketing image to
28 showcase Iowa’s workforce, existing industry, and potential.
29 The authority shall prioritize recruitment of new businesses,
30 business expansion, and retention of existing Iowa businesses.
31 In addition, the authority shall emphasize entrepreneurial
32 development through helping entrepreneurs secure capital,
33 and developing networks and a business climate conducive to
34 entrepreneurs and small businesses.
35 g. Encourage the development of communities and quality of

1 life to foster economic growth.
2 h. Prepare communities for future growth and development
3 through development, expansion, and modernization of
4 infrastructure.
5 i. Develop public-private partnerships with Iowa businesses
6 in the tourism industry, Iowa tour groups, Iowa tourism
7 organizations, and political subdivisions in this state to
8 assist in the development of advertising efforts.
9 j. Collaborate with Iowa small businesses and communities

15 Sec. 12. NEW SECTION. 16.52 Housing renewal program.
16 1. For purposes of this section, unless the context
17 otherwise requires:
18 a. “Eligible participant” includes cities, counties,
19 consortiums of local governments, and organizations exempt from
20 taxation pursuant to section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue
21 Code.
22 b. “Nonprofit Iowa affiliate” means a nonprofit Iowa
23 affiliate of a nonprofit international organization whose
24 primary activity is the promotion of the construction,
25 remodeling, or rehabilitation of one-family or two-family
26 dwellings for use by low-income families.
27 2. a. A housing renewal program fund is created in the
28 state treasury under the control of the authority. The fund
29 shall consist of moneys appropriated to or deposited in the
30 fund. Moneys in the fund are appropriated to the authority to
31 administer the housing renewal program.
32 b. Notwithstanding section 8.33, moneys appropriated to
33 the housing renewal program fund that remain uncumbered or
34 unobligated at the close of a fiscal year shall not revert
35 but shall remain available for expenditure for the purposes

2 c. Notwithstanding section 12C.7, subsection 2, interest or
3 earnings on moneys in the housing renewal program fund shall be
4 credited to the fund. Payment of interest, income generated
5 from the sale of an ownership unit pursuant to subsection 6,
6 recaptures of financial assistance awards, and other repayments

9 assistance under the housing renewal program to eligible

11 3. a. The authority shall provide moneys from the housing
12 renewal program fund to a nonprofit Iowa affiliate that shall
13 use the moneys to award financial assistance under the housing
14 renewal program to eligible participants.
15 b. Eligible participants shall use financial assistance
16 awarded under the housing renewal program only for purposes of
17 the acquisition, rehabilitation, and resale of ownership units;
18 the acquisition and demolition of blighted structures; and the
19 redevelopment of ownership units.
20 4. Twenty-five percent of moneys appropriated to the
21 housing renewal program fund shall be allocated to financial
22 assistance awards for eligible participants located in the
23 eighty-eight least populated counties in the state.
24 5. a. The nonprofit Iowa affiliate shall determine the
25 criteria used to evaluate eligible participants and to award
26 financial assistance to eligible participants selected by the
27 nonprofit Iowa affiliate.
28 b. The nonprofit Iowa affiliate shall not award an eligible
29 participant more than one hundred thousand dollars per
30 ownership unit. The nonprofit Iowa affiliate may use up to
31 five percent of the financial assistance awarded to an eligible
32 participant for administrative expenses related to the housing
33 renewal program.
34 c. (1) An eligible participant shall have a maximum of
35 thirty-six months from the date a contract is executed between
