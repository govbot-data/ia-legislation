11 EXPLANATION
12 The inclusion of this explanation does not constitute agreement with
13 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
14 This bill relates to career education, including by
15 modifying provisions related to career academies, the
16 collective statewide articulation agreement between
17 community colleges and the state board of regents, the
18 district-to-community college sharing or concurrent enrollment
19 program, and individual career and academic plans.
20 Under current law, a career academy may be established under
21 an agreement between a single school district and a community
22 college, or by multiple school districts and a community
23 college organized into a regional career and technical
24 education planning partnership, and such a career academy is
25 required to be a career-oriented or occupation-oriented program
26 of study that includes a minimum of two years of secondary
27 education. The bill provides that a career academy is required
28 to include a minimum of two units of secondary education, of
29 which at least one unit must be from a course that meets the
30 requirements of Code section 261E.8 (district-to-community
31 college sharing or concurrent enrollment program).
32 Current law requires the boards of directors of community
33 colleges to enter into a collective statewide articulation
34 agreement with the state board of regents that provides for
35 the seamless transfer of academic credits from a completed

1 associate of arts or associate of science degree program
2 offered by a community college to a baccalaureate degree
3 program offered by an institution of higher education governed
4 by the state board of regents. The bill modifies this
5 provision to provide that the collective statewide articulation
6 agreement must provide for the seamless transfer of academic
7 credits earned through a completed associate of arts degree

11 of science degree program, an applied associate of arts degree
12 program, or an applied associate of science degree program
13 to a baccalaureate degree program offered by an institution
14 of higher education governed by the state board of regents.
15 Additionally, the collective statewide articulation agreement
16 is required to include program-specific arrangements that
17 favor the transfer of career and technical education credits
18 unless extenuating circumstances prevent such transfer and is
19 prohibited from including a uniform restriction related to the
20 number of career and technical education credits that may be
21 transferred.
