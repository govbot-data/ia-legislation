11 EXPLANATION
12 The inclusion of this explanation does not constitute agreement with
13 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
14 This bill relates to preneed sellers, sales agents and sales
15 agent applicants, and government cemeteries.
16 The bill amends Code sections 523A.201(8) (establishment
17 of trust funds), 523A.602(2)(b)(1) (consumer rescission,
18 cancellation, and refund rights —— purchase agreement
19 compliance with other laws), and 523A.602(2)(c) to calculate
20 the rate of inflation by relying on the most recently released
21 consumer price index for all urban consumers published by the
22 United States department of labor, bureau of labor statistics,
23 rather than relying on the inflation as set by the commissioner
24 of insurance (commissioner) based on the consumer price index
25 under current law.
26 The bill eliminates the requirement under current law that
27 if a preneed seller has a trust agreement, the commissioner
28 shall conduct an examination of the preneed seller at least
29 once every five years unless the preneed seller has annually
30 provided to the commissioner a certified copy of an audit
31 conducted by an independent certified public accountant. Under
32 the bill, the commissioner may require a preneed seller to
33 provide a certified copy of an audit of the preneed seller
34 conducted by an independent certified public accountant to
35 verify compliance.

26 under current law.

29 than once every five years of any cemetery that has a trust
