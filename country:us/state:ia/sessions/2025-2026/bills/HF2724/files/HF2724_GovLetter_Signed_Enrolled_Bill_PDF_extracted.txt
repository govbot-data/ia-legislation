House File 2724
AN ACT
REQUIRING THE BOARD OF EDUCATIONAL EXAMINERS TO ESTABLISH
ADDITIONAL ENDORSEMENTS AND AUTHORIZATIONS.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA:
Section 1. Section 256.146, Code 2026, is amended by adding
the following new subsections:
NEW SUBSECTION. 28, a. Adopt rules pursuant to chapter 17A
establishing additional endorsements for instruction related
to all of the following high-need teaching areas in schools
providing instruction to students enrolled in prekindergarten
through grade twelve:
(1) Fine arts.
(2) Mathematics.
(3) Science.
b. The board shall not grant an applicant an endorsement
described in paragraph unless the applicant holds a
bachelor's degree from an accredited college or university,
c. This subsection is repealed July 1, 2030.
NEW SUBSECTION. 29. Adopt rules pursuant to chapter 17A
establishing an additional kindergarten through grade twelve
instructional strategist II endorsement that does not require
the applicant to possess a master's or doctoral degree as a
condition of obtaining the endorsement.
NEW SUBSECTION, 30. Adopt rules pursuant to chapter 17A
establishing an activities administration authorization.
The rules must require an applicant for the activities

House File 2724, p. 2
administration authorization to hold a baccalaureate degree or
higher in any academic field.
