3 This bill relates to victim protections including victim
4 counselors, protective orders, sex offender registration,
5 rights of sexual abuse victims, mental competency and civil
6 commitment, restitution, and victim rights.
7 DIVISION I. The bill provides that a victim counselor, or a
8 clerk, secretary, stenographer, or any other employee who types
9 or otherwise prepares or manages the confidential reports or
10 working papers of a victim counselor, must not be compelled to
11 produce confidential communications or confidential information
12 or be examined or required to give evidence concerning any
13 confidential communication or confidential information made by
14 a victim to the counselor, except under any of the following
15 circumstances: the victim waives the privilege in writing,
16 disclosure of the information is compelled by a court, or
17 failure to disclose is likely to result in a clear, imminent
18 risk of serious physical injury or death of the victim or
19 another person.
20 The bill provides that a victim counselor or crime victim
21 center participating in good faith in the disclosing of records
22 and communications under the bill shall have immunity from any
23 liability, civil or criminal, arising out of a disclosure under
24 the bill. The good faith of the victim counselor or crime
25 victim center shall be presumed.
26 DIVISION II. The bill provides that in the sentencing
27 proceeding for a person convicted of certain sexual offenses a
28 prosecutor may request that the criminal court grant the victim
29 an order of protection to remain in effect for the duration of
30 the criminal court’s jurisdiction over the person. After the
31 expiration of a criminal court’s jurisdiction over a person
32 against whom an order of protection was granted pursuant to a
33 request under this paragraph, the victim may file a petition
34 for an order of protection against the person, or submit
35 evidence of the person’s conviction for a sexual offense as
