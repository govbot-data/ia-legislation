24 EXPLANATION
25 The inclusion of this explanation does not constitute agreement with
26 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
27 This bill relates to matters under the purview of the
28 department of inspections, appeals, and licensing. The bill is
29 organized in divisions.
30 DIVISION I —— GAMBLING. The bill eliminates a
31 $30,000-per-year total cap on reimbursements that the state
32 racing and gaming commission (commission) may pay to all
33 commissioners for expenses incurred in the performance of the
34 commissioners’ duties. The bill grants the commission explicit
35 authority to issue cease and desist orders and seek injunctive

1 relief against persons who offer gambling services in the state
2 without an appropriate license or authorization.
3 Under current law, a person commits a class “D” felony
4 and is barred for life from excursion gambling boats and
5 gambling structures under the jurisdiction of the commission
6 for committing certain acts listed in Code section 99F.15(4).
7 The bill expands the lifetime ban to include sports wagering
8 and advance deposit sports wagering. The bill also imposes a
9 lifetime ban from sports wagering and advance deposit sports
10 wagering on a person who is convicted twice of unlawful
11 betting.
12 DIVISION II —— STATE BUILDING CODE. The bill requires the
13 state building code commissioner to consult with the department
14 of homeland security and emergency management, instead of
15 the department of public defense, in adopting rules relating
16 to safe rooms and storm shelters. The bill also requires
17 fees collected by the state building code commissioner to be
18 deposited in the licensing and regulation fund instead of the
19 general fund of the state.
20 DIVISION III —— RESIDENTIAL CARE FACILITIES. The bill
21 allows a residential care facility that is a pediatric
22 palliative care center to serve as few as zero individuals.
23 Under current law, an entity must serve at least three
24 individuals to be defined as a residential care facility.
