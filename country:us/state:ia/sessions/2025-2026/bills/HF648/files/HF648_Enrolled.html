NEW SECTION . 153.25 Licensed sedation provider host permit. 1. The board may issue a licensed sedation provider host permit to a dentist who does all of the following: a. Completes a course approved by the board as described in subsection 3 within the twelve months immediately preceding the submission of the application, or provide evidence that the dentist holds a substantially similar permit issued in another state.
House File 648, p. 2 b. Meets the facility and equipment requirements as determined by the board by rule. c. Receives certification in administering advanced cardiovascular life support as determined by the board by rule. d. Submits an application in a form prescribed by the board. 2. A dentist who holds a licensed sedation provider host permit may do all of the following: a. Employ a licensed sedation provider, or a dentist who holds a current moderate sedation or general anesthesia permit issued by the board, to administer moderate sedation on an outpatient basis to a patient who is at least thirteen years of age on whom the dentist is performing a dental procedure. b. Monitor a patient who is at least thirteen years of age who is under moderate sedation. 3. a. The board shall only approve a licensed sedation provider host permit course that provides instruction and an assessment of knowledge and skill in the following areas: (1) Preoperative patient assessment. (2) Emergency scenarios and rescue. (3) Respiratory complication. (4) Patient safety and monitoring. (5) Airway assessment and management. (6) Anesthetic drugs. (7) Recovery. b.
