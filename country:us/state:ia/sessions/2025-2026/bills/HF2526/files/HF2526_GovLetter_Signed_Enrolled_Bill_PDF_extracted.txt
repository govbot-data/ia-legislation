House File 2526
AN ACT
RELATING TO THE CONFIDENTIALITY OF THE RESIDENTIAL ADDRESSES OF
FOSTER PARENTS.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA:
Section 1. NEW SECTION. 237D.1 Poster parent addresses —
confidentiality.
1. For purposes of this chapter:
a, ^Address" means the residential address of a person.
b, ^''Child foster care" means the same as defined in section
237.1.
c, "^Department" means the department of health and human
services.
d, ""^Foster child" means a child receiving child foster care
e, '^Foster parent" means an individual licensee as defined
in section 237.1.
2. a* The department and the department's agents shall
not knowingly provide or make available a document to a
biological parent of a foster child if the document contains
the unredacted address of the foster parent who is currently
providing child foster care to the foster child.
b. The department and the department's agents shall not
verbally disclose the address of the foster parent who is
currently providing child foster care to a foster child to a
biological parent of the foster child.
3. Notwithstanding subsection 2, a document may contain a
foster parent's unredacted address, and the department and the

department's agents may verbally disclose a foster parent's
address, if the foster parent provides written consent to
disclose the address to a foster child's biological parent
prior to the department or the department's agent providing the
document, making the document available, or verbally disclosing
the address to the biological parent.
