5 Sec. ___. SPECIAL EDUCATION WEIGHTING WORK GROUP.
6 1. School funding in Iowa has not evolved as the
7 demographics of Iowa students have changed significantly, with
8 little attention paid to supporting the special education needs
9 of students. In order to meet the needs of such students and
10 to ensure access to special education across school districts,
11 the special education weighting work group shall review the
12 special education weighting plan under section 256B.9, which
13 has not been altered since 2004, and shall consider all of the
14 following:
15 a. How to increase special education weightings in a way
16 that both meets the needs of students and minimizes the need
17 for school districts to increase property taxes.
18 b. The standards associated with each special education
19 weighting.
20 c. Best practices in the United States related to funding
21 special education.
22 2. The school finance review committee met on December
23 16, 2024, and received a memo from the school budget review
24 committee that discussed how a review of special education
25 funding should be conducted.
26 3. The director of the department of education shall
27 establish and appoint members to a work group to make
28 recommendations to modify the special education weighting plan.
29 The department of education shall work cooperatively to provide
30 staffing assistance for the work group. The work group shall
31 be composed of at least all of the following:
32 a. One member representing the Iowa association of school
33 boards.
34 b. One member representing the area education agencies.
