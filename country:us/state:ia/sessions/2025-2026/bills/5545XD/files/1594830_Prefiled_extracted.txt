2 EXPLANATION
3 The inclusion of this explanation does not constitute agreement with
4 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
5 This bill relates to the safety of persons in certain
6 professions involved with the judicial system, including by
7 authorizing a judicial officer, attorney general, deputy
8 attorney general, and assistant attorney general to be issued
9 a professional permit to carry weapons; and establishing the
10 criminal offenses of threatening and the malicious sharing of
11 personal information of a judicial officer or a member of the
12 judicial officer’s immediate family.
13 DIVISION I. The bill provides that a permit issued to a
14 judicial officer, attorney general, deputy attorney general,
15 or assistant attorney general grants authorization to go armed
16 anywhere in the state at all times, including on the grounds
17 of a school.
18 The bill provides that a permit issued to a judicial
19 officer, attorney general, deputy attorney general, or
20 assistant attorney general is valid through the holder of the
21 permit’s period of employment unless otherwise canceled. When
22 the employment is terminated, the holder of the permit is
23 required to surrender the permit to the issuing officer for
24 cancellation.
25 The bill defines “judicial officer” as a supreme court
26 justice, a judge of the court of appeals, a district judge,
27 a district associate judge, an associate juvenile judge, an
28 associate probate judge, or a magistrate. The term also
29 includes a person who is temporarily serving as a justice,
30 judge, or magistrate as permitted by law.
31 The bill allows the supreme court to impose additional
32 training or other requirements on judicial officers who hold a
33 permit issued under the bill.
34 DIVISION II. The bill establishes the offense of
35 threatening a judicial officer or an immediate family member

1 of a judicial officer.
2 The bill prohibits a person from threatening a judicial
3 officer or the immediate family member of a judicial officer
4 with the intent to do any of the following: placing the
5 judicial officer or the immediate family member of a judicial
6 officer in fear of serious injury to the judicial officer or
7 the immediate family member of the judicial officer; preventing
8 or interrupting the ability of the judicial officer to carry
9 out the judicial officer’s job duties; or retaliating against a
10 judicial officer in relation to the performance of the judicial
11 officer’s official duties during the judicial officer’s term
12 of service. The bill defines “immediate family member” and
13 “retaliation”.
14 A person violating this division of the bill is guilty
15 of a class “C” felony. A class “C” felony is punishable by
16 confinement for no more than 10 years and a fine of at least
17 $1,370 but not more than $13,660.
18 DIVISION III. The bill establishes the criminal offense of
19 the malicious sharing of personal information of a judicial
20 officer or an immediate family member of a judicial officer.
21 The bill prohibits a person from sharing the personal
22 information of a judicial officer or the immediate family
23 member of a judicial officer with the intent to do any of
24 the following: causing harm to the judicial officer or the
25 immediate family member of a judicial officer; placing the
26 judicial officer or the immediate family member of a judicial
27 officer in fear of serious harm to the judicial officer
28 or to an immediate family member of the judicial officer;
29 or preventing or interrupting the ability to carry out the
30 judicial officer’s job duties. The bill defines “personal
31 information”.
32 A person violating this division of the bill is guilty of a
33 serious misdemeanor. A serious misdemeanor is punishable by
34 confinement for no more than one year and a fine of at least
35 $430 but not more than $2,560.
