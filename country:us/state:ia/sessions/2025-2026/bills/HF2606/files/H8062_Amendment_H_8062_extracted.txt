16 Sec. 2. NEW SECTION. 554J.2 Age bracket data requirements.
17 1. An operating system provider shall do all of the
18 following:
19 a. Provide an accessible interface at account setup that
20 requires an account holder to indicate the birth date, age, or
21 both, of the user of that device for the purpose of providing a
22 signal regarding the user’s age bracket data to apps available
23 in a covered app store.
24 b. Provide a developer who has requested a signal with
25 respect to a particular user with a digital signal via a
26 reasonably consistent real-time application programming
27 interface that identifies, at a minimum, the age range of age
28 bracket data to which the user belongs.
29 c. Send only the minimum amount of information necessary to
30 comply with this chapter.
31 d. Refrain from sharing digital signal information with a
32 third party for a purpose not required by this chapter.
33 2. a. A developer shall request a signal with respect to a
34 particular user from an operating system provider or a covered
35 app store when the developer’s app is downloaded and launched.
