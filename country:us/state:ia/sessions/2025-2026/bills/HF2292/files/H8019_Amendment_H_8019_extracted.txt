10 having regularly used hard drugs like methamphetamines,
11 cocaine, or opioids, and an equally large share of homeless

18 4. The state needs to create space for many homeless
19 individuals in a long-term institutional setting to provide the
20 humane treatment these individuals need to address the root
21 cause of homelessness and restore public order.

28 mental diseases exclusion.
29 2. No later than one calendar year after the centers for
