House File 2303
AN ACT
RELATING TO REGULATION OF PROFESSIONAL AND AMATEUR KICKBOXING
BY THE STATE COMMISSIONER OF ATHLETICS.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA:
Section 1. Section 90A.1, Code 2026, is amended by adding
the following new subsection:
NEW SUBSECTION. 2A. "Kickboxing match" means a professional
or amateur kickboxing match or event that is open to the public
and an admission fee is charged, a donation is requested
from those in attendance, or merchandise or refreshments are
available for purchase.
Sec. 2. Section 90A.1, subsection 9, Code 2026, is amended
by adding the following new paragraph:
NEW PARAGRAPH, d. Organizes, holds, advertises, or
otherwise conducts a kickboxing match.
Sec. 3. Section 90A.2, subsection 1, Code 2026, is amended
to read as follows:
1. A person shall not act as a promoter of a professional
boxing or wrestling matchy e-r- a mixed martial arts match, or
a kickboxing match without first obtaining a license from the
commissioner. This subsection shall not apply to a person
distributing a closed-circuit, pay-per-view, or similarly
distributed signal to a person acting as a promoter or to a
person viewing the signal in a private residence.
