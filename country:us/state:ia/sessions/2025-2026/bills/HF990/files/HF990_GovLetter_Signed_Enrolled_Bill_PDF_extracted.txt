House File 990
AN ACT
RELATING TO THE ISSUANCE OF MEDICAL CANNABIDIOL REGISTRATION
CARDS AND LICENSURE OF MEDICAL CANNABIDIOL DISPENSARIES.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA:
Section 1. Section 124E.4, subsection 1, paragraph b, Code
2026, is amended by striking the paragraph.
Sec. 2. Section 124E.4, subsection 1, paragraph d,
subparagraph (1), Code 2026, is amended to read as follows:
(1) The patient's full name, Iowa residence address, date
of birth, and telephone number.
Sec. 3. Section 124E.4, subsection 2, paragraph a, Code
2026, is amended to read as follows:
a. The patient's full name, T-owa residence address, and date
of birth.
Sec. 4. Section 124E.8, subsection 1, paragraph a, Code
2026, is amended to read as follows:
a. The department shall issue a request for- proposals accept
applications to select and license by April 1,—2018,—up to five
medical cannabidiol dispensaries , and may select and license up
to ten medical cannabidiol dispensaries, to dispense medical
cannabidiol within this state consistent with the provisions
of this chapter. The department shall license new medical
cannabidiol dispensaries or relicense the existing medical
cannabidiol dispensaries by December 1 of each year.
