Senate File 2480
H-8468
1 Amend Senate File 2480, as amended, passed, and reprinted by
2 the Senate, as follows:
3 1. By striking everything after the enacting clause and
4 inserting:
5 <Section 1. NEW SECTION. 135.196 Pediatric cancer research
6 —— appropriation.
7 For the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2026, and for each
8 fiscal year thereafter, there is appropriated from the general
9 fund of the state to the state board of regents an amount
10 equal to one dollar per resident of the state on July 1 of the
11 same fiscal year, based on the most recent annual population
12 estimate issued by the United States census bureau, not to
13 exceed three million dollars per fiscal year, to be used
14 for pediatric cancer research including but not limited to
15 laboratory research and clinical trials at the university of
16 Iowa hospitals and clinics. Moneys appropriated under this
17 section shall not be used for administrative or overhead
18 costs, or activities not directly related to pediatric cancer
19 research. The state board of regents shall submit an annual
20 report to the governor and the general assembly by October
21 1 detailing how the appropriated moneys were used in the
22 immediately preceding fiscal year.>
23 2. Title page, by striking lines 1 through 3 and inserting
24 <An Act providing a standing appropriation to the state board
25 of regents for pediatric cancer research at the university of
26 Iowa hospitals and clinics.>
