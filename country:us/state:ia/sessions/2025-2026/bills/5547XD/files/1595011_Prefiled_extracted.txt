16 EXPLANATION
17 The inclusion of this explanation does not constitute agreement with
18 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
19 This bill increases the maximum amount of reimbursement
20 to the attorney general annually from the second injury fund
21 for workers’ compensation for services provided related to the
22 fund from $450,000 to $900,000. The bill provides that the
23 reimbursement amount shall be adjusted annually to reflect any
24 increase in the consumer price index, rounded to the nearest
25 $5, since the previous reimbursement.
