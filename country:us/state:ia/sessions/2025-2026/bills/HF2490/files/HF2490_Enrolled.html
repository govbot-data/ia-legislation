Sec. 4. NEW SECTION . 22.8A Injunction to restrain vexatious requester. 1. The district court may grant an injunction restraining the right of a person found to be a vexatious requester to examine public records under section 22.2 from a specific government body, or may impose reasonable limitations on the manner, frequency, or scope of such requests.
