20 EXPLANATION
21 The inclusion of this explanation does not constitute agreement with
22 the explanation’s substance by the members of the general assembly.
23 This bill relates to activities, programs, and services
24 under the purview of the department of health and human
25 services (HHS).
26 DIVISION I —— HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES DISTRICTS. The bill
27 directs HHS to divide the state into health and human services
28 districts (districts), as defined in the bill, to be used by
29 HHS in the delivery of programs and services. The initial
30 districts shall be the same as the behavioral health districts
31 designated in Code section 225A.4, Code 2026, as the behavioral
32 health districts exist on the effective date of this division
33 of the bill. The bill provides factors HHS must consider when
34 modifying the districts. The manner in which HHS modifies a
35 district is not subject to judicial review.

1 The bill changes Code chapter 225A (HHS —— behavioral health
2 service system) so that behavioral health services are provided
3 through the health and human services districts instead of
4 behavioral health districts.
5 The bill makes conforming changes to Code section 12.51.
6 This division of the bill takes effect upon enactment.
7 DIVISION II —— AGING AND DISABILITY SERVICES. Under current
8 law, HHS is required to divide the state into distinct planning
9 and service areas based on various factors for the purpose of
10 providing aging and disability services. The bill requires
11 HHS to designate each planning and service area (service area)
12 throughout the state in accordance with federal requirements.
13 The boundaries of service areas shall match the boundaries of
14 districts.
15 This division of the bill takes effect upon enactment
16 and applies upon HHS’s receipt, from the administration for
17 community living of the United States department of health and
18 human services, of approval of the planning and service areas
19 as amended in the bill.
20 DIVISION III —— VOLUNTEER SERVICES. The bill creates a
21 subunit for volunteerism (subunit) within HHS. The subunit
22 shall establish volunteer programs designed to enhance the
23 services provided by HHS and provide administrative support
24 for the nonpolicymaking duties of the Iowa commission on
25 volunteer service (commission). The director of HHS shall
26 appoint an executive director to oversee the administration of
27 the subunit and to carry out nonpolicymaking duties delegated
28 to the subunit by the commission. HHS may employ personnel
29 for the subunit to carry out the duties of the subunit and
30 the duties delegated to the subunit by the commission. All
31 volunteers registered with programs administered by the
32 subunit are considered employees of the state; however, this
33 does not exempt a volunteer conservator or guardian from the
34 conservator’s or guardian’s duties and does not limit the
35 conservator’s or guardian’s civil liability for performing the
